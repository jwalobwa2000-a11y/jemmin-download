How to install
  1. Tap the button for your app. If Chrome asks, tap Download.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open.
  3. If Android says it cannot install from this source, tap Settings and switch on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. The app is safe; it is not on the Play Store yet.
  5. Open Jemmin, create your account with your phone number and a 6-digit PIN.
